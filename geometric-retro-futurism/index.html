Motion

M-006 / Tokens · Principles

Motion is mechanical and quiet. Things slide on rails, orbit on ellipses and settle without bounce. Press Run on a specimen, then press again mid-flight to watch it retarget instead of restarting.

Ambient · pauses off-screenRunning
  1. Transitions for stateHover, toggles and tab indicators use CSS transitions, so they can be interrupted and retargeted mid-flight.
  2. Keyframes for ambienceOrbits, marquees, twinkles and foil drift loop quietly in the background and never block input.
  3. Enter louder than exitEnter with a 12 to 16px rise and a 4px blur. Exit with 8px in half the time.
  4. No bounceMechanical ease-out curves only. Things land, they don't wobble.
  5. Press is 0.96Every pressable surface scales to 96% or seats its offset shadow back into the page.
  6. Respect stillnessprefers-reduced-motion collapses all motion into instant state changes.
